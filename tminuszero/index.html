// The launch sequence

From idea to orbit in five stages

Our method for every engagement. Each stage has clear exit criteria, so you always know what “ready” means.

  1. T−4

    Mission brief

    Define the use case, its purpose, users, data and the business outcome it must deliver.

  2. T−3

    Risk assessment

    Classify the system, identify harms and impacts, and rate the risk against your appetite.

  3. T−2

    Controls & compliance

    Map obligations, put the right controls in place and document the evidence.

  4. T−1

    Readiness review

    Test, evaluate and poll every station. Independent go / no-go recommendation.

  5. T−0

    Launch & monitor

    Go live with monitoring, incident response and periodic re-review built in.
