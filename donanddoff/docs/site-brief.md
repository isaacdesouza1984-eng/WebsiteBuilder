# Don & Doff Co. — Website Brief

## Brand overview
Don & Doff Co. is a space-themed apparel brand. The name comes from real
spaceflight terminology: "donning" (putting on a spacesuit) and "doffing"
(taking it off) — the two bookends of every mission. The term has been in
NASA's technical vocabulary since the Gemini program (mid-1960s), was drilled
into procedure by Apollo, and is still part of NASA's human spaceflight
standards today (NASA-STD-3001 includes a formal donning/doffing
requirement).

Brand positioning: a heritage spacewear outfitter — as if it's an old
outfitter's guild that's dressed travelers for departure and return for
generations. Not sleek sci-fi minimalism, not retro-NASA either. Think old
spacefaring guild crest, founders' seal, worn brass and starlight — a brand
that feels like it predates the mission it's outfitting you for.

## Tagline
"Suit up. Reach orbit. Repeat."

## Voice
Confident, spare, ritual-like. Every phrase should read like a checklist
item, not a slogan. No exclamation points, no hype-brand energy. Short
sentences. Let the name and the real NASA terminology carry the weight
instead of over-explaining.

## Visual identity
Two logo lockups exist (circular crest / mission-patch hybrid, both in
uploaded PNG form — "Heritage" and "Agency" versions):

- **Heritage version**: aged bronze/brass tones on dark charcoal
  (#c9a25c, #9a7a3f, #6b5732 on #1b1a17 / #2a2822). Central emblem is a
  symmetrical orbital-ring/astrolabe motif with a small globe, flanked by
  branching antenna forms standing in for laurel leaves. Arced wordmark
  "DON & DOFF CO." along the top, "SUIT UP · REACH ORBIT · REPEAT" arced
  along the bottom, "EST. JD [julian date]" centered above the emblem.
- **Agency version**: same layout and emblem, recolored in NASA blue/red/white
  (#0b3d91 blue, #fc3d21 red, white field) for a crisper, more official feel.

Use the Heritage version as the primary/default brand mark; Agency version as
a secondary palette for specific drops or accents.

Typography direction: wide letter-spacing, engraved/etched character — like
it was stamped into metal, not printed. Avoid rounded, playful, or
tech-startup-style type.

## Products
- Shirts and hoodies with space-themed designs (AI-generated imagery,
  restrained/painterly rather than glossy sci-fi render style).
- Flagship design concept: a t-shirt styled like spacesuit construction —
  contrast piping/seam lines running through the shoulders and torso,
  evoking a pressure suit without being a literal costume. (Reference image
  available — black tee with white/grey contrast line work tracing seams
  across the chest and down the torso.)
- Decided production approach for the piping-effect shirt: **printed**
  (DTG/DTF on a premium blank), not real sewn piping — cut-and-sew was
  evaluated and set aside for v1 due to cost/MOQ/lead-time risk.
- Target retail price for the flagship tee: **~$99**, aiming for roughly
  72–82% margin against an estimated $18–28 production cost (premium blank +
  DTG/DTF print, sourced from a Toronto printer).

## Launch model: threshold pre-order
Launch plan is a pre-order campaign with a minimum unit threshold to trigger
production (de-risks inventory):
- Target threshold: 50 units to start.
- Campaign window: 2–3 weeks, fixed end date (not open-ended).
- Payment model: charge at order, full refund to everyone if threshold isn't
  met by the deadline. Be explicit and transparent about this on the
  product/campaign page — state the threshold, deadline, refund terms, and
  estimated ship date if the threshold is hit.
- Platform: Shopify.

## What the site needs (v1 scope)
- Homepage that establishes the brand story/voice quickly — the
  donning/doffing origin, the tagline, the crest logo — without over-
  explaining. Should feel like a mission-patch / heritage-outfitter site,
  not a generic streetwear drop site.
- Product page for the flagship tee, built around the pre-order/threshold
  model described above: price, countdown to campaign end date, progress
  toward the 50-unit threshold, clear refund terms.
- About section/page using the brand story (Gemini → Apollo → modern NASA
  standard) as the "why the name" narrative.
- Instagram link/handle integration (brand is already posting as
  @donanddoff or similar — confirm handle).
- Clean, dark, crest-forward aesthetic matching the logo palette above.
  Avoid generic Shopify-template look; avoid bright/neon sci-fi styling.
