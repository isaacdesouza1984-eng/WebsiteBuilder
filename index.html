Our story

Authentic Persian food, made with care

At Afraa Restaurant we bring you the flavours of Iran: juicy koobideh, tender lamb shank, slow-cooked traditional stews and rice with a golden tahdig, all prepared fresh with premium ingredients.

Inspired by age-old recipes and prepared with the finest ingredients, each dish tells a story of Persian heritage. Known for generous portions, a warm atmosphere and exceptional hospitality, Afraa is a favourite destination for Persian cuisine. Whether you're dining in, picking up or ordering delivery, every visit is a memorable meal.
