Our story

Authentic Persian food, made with care

At Afraa Restaurant we bring you the flavours of Iran: juicy koobideh, tender lamb shank, slow-cooked traditional stews and rice with a golden tahdig, all prepared fresh with premium ingredients.

Known for generous portions, a warm atmosphere and exceptional hospitality, Afraa is one of the region's favourite destinations for Persian cuisine. Every table starts with soup, bread and cheese, and ends with free tea and cookies.
